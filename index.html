THE PROBLEM

Problems in the community we want to solve.

NGOs and communities often face communication, coordination and accessibility problems. TechSahayog brings these solutions together.

📢

Lack of Online Presence

NGOs often don't have one place to showcase their activities, projects and achievements.

→ Solution: Digital NGO platform
👥

Volunteer Management

Volunteer information can be scattered across registers, spreadsheets and messaging apps.

→ Solution: Online volunteer registration
🆘

People Don't Know Where to Ask for Help

People may not know whom to contact when they need educational, financial or community support.

→ Solution: Get Help system
💰

Donation Accessibility

Donors need an easy way to understand causes and contribute towards community projects.

→ Solution: Digital donation interface
📅

Scattered Events

Community events can be difficult to discover and register for.

→ Solution: Central event section
🔗

Volunteers & People Are Disconnected

People willing to help and people requiring support need a common platform.

→ Solution: Community matching
